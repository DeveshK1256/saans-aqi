What Saans does

Five features that turn vague worry into clear, specific action.

1

Household Risk Profile

2-minute setup. Tell us who lives with you, your home type, and how you commute. Every recommendation is weighted by your most vulnerable family member.

2

Personal Exposure Score

Not just a city AQI — an estimated daily PM2.5 dose for each person, with cigarette-equivalents you can actually feel. Source, station, and freshness shown for every number.

3

What-If Simulator

Move sliders — see your dose drop instantly. Close windows during cooking? Add a DIY purifier? The numbers update in real time, right on your phone, even offline.

4

DIY Purifier Guide + Myth Buster

Build a Corsi-Rosenthal box for under ₹2,000. Paste any WhatsApp forward and get an evidence-rated verdict with real citations — not "trust me."

5

Neighborhood Clean-Air Pledge

Your housing society pledges to cut emissions. The leaderboard shows your collective impact — no individual data exposed, all self-reported and labeled honestly.

What Saans is not

  • Not a medical device or a doctor's replacement
  • Not a real-time indoor air sensor — indoor values are modeled estimates
  • Not an emergency alert system (but it shows emergency numbers when symptoms are reported)
  • Not a data broker — we never sell or share your data

1. The Inversion Ceiling (Why Winter Smog Traps)

During clear winter nights, urban concrete and roads radiate heat into the atmosphere rapidly, creating a cold, dense surface layer. Warmer air aloft acts as a physical ceiling (Planetary Boundary Layer). Emissions cannot penetrate this barrier and pool within the bottom 200–300 meters.

2. Street Canyon Stratification

Tall buildings along narrow roads create "urban street canyons" that trap heavy vehicular exhaust (NOx, elemental carbon, tire wear). Ground-floor and 1st-floor residents inhale 2.4× higher particulate peaks than residents on higher floors (6th+), who sit above the primary shear layer.

3. Indoor Decoupling (Nazaroff 2004)

Indoor air quality is governed by the mass balance differential equation: λ = a + k + Qp/V. When windows are weather-stripped (low infiltration a) and an air purifier runs (high CADR Qp), the room decouples from ambient outdoor pollution, reducing inhaled dose by 70–85%.

🚨 Critical Respiratory Symptom Escalation

Saans is an environmental exposure calculator, never a doctor or medical emergency device. If you, your child, or an elderly family member experience any of the following acute symptoms, seek immediate emergency medical care:

  • ⚠️ Audible wheezing or whistling sound when breathing in or out
  • ⚠️ Inability to complete full sentences without gasping for breath
  • ⚠️ Chest tightness, pain, or pressure radiating to arm or jaw
  • ⚠️ Bluish tint on lips, fingernails, or tongue (cyanosis / low oxygen)
  • ⚠️ Child's ribs pulling in deeply during breathing (retractions)
Do not rely on indoor air purifiers, steam, or herbal teas during acute breathing distress.

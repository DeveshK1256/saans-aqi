🚨 Critical Respiratory Symptom Escalation

Saans is an environmental exposure calculator, never a doctor or medical emergency device. If you, your child, or an elderly family member experience any of the following acute symptoms, seek immediate emergency medical care:

  • ⚠️ Audible wheezing or whistling sound when breathing in or out
  • ⚠️ Inability to complete full sentences without gasping for breath
  • ⚠️ Chest tightness, pain, or pressure radiating to arm or jaw
  • ⚠️ Bluish tint on lips, fingernails, or tongue (cyanosis / low oxygen)
  • ⚠️ Child's ribs pulling in deeply during breathing (retractions)
Do not rely on indoor air purifiers, steam, or herbal teas during acute breathing distress.
